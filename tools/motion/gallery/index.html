Onboarding · Round 1

Purr's first hello, in two flavours

Liquid-morph studies for screens 1.1 and 1.2 and the intro fly-over. Each decision has an A and a B. Pick one of each below, and the bar at the bottom writes your reply for you. The welcome screen's parts mix and match, so you can take A's button with B's tagline.

1.1 Launch→Head melts into six dots→Logo and name→1.2 Welcome→Fly-over→1.3 Quiz
1.1

Launch frame

The system launch screen becomes this light lavender, so the hand-off is invisible. Purr's head is about 190 points wide, roughly two and a half times BitePal's.

A

Peekaboo

Purr rises from behind a rippling liquid horizon, flicking up droplets that fall back in. Squash, stretch and a tilt as he settles.

B

Drip & pop

A violet drop falls and splashes into a wobbling blob, and Purr pops out of it with an overshoot. The blob settles into a soft halo and three sparkles twinkle.

1.1 → 1.2

Head → six dots → logo

The head melts into a drop that splits into six dots, Cosmos style. In the app the dots keep turning only while the app really is loading, then form the brand. The name is set in Fredoka.

A

Orbit merge

The six dots orbit and pulse in turn, then pull together into the logo tile. The street of homes builds itself, Purr hops in, and "Purrperty" springs up letter by letter.

B

Paw print

Five dots regroup into a paw print. The sixth stretches into a coral underline, and the name rises out from behind it.

1.2

Welcome

Feature cards drift above and below the name, in opposite directions. They show only things the app does today: recorded rent, repairs, renewals, vacancies, tenant chat, the Rent widget, the visit Live Activity, vendor jobs and the Ontario rent check. Each video ends with a press of the main button.

A

Orb, line, sleepy Purr

  • Button: a liquid orb with an arrow and a violet-to-coral gradient. Its edge ripples; pressed, it stretches and floods the screen.
  • Tagline: "Every rental, under one paw."
  • Terms: one muted line under the button, Bump style.
  • Sign in: a glass pill with a sleeping Purr who wakes up when tapped.
B

Paw fill, chip, door

  • Button: a glass capsule with Purr's paw. Holding it fills it with liquid, then it bursts.
  • Tagline: "Your rentals, purring along."
  • Terms: a small glass "Terms · Privacy" chip with a caption above it.
  • Sign in: a "Have an account?" pill whose little door swings open.
After the button

Intro fly-over

About nine seconds, muted, with a Skip button, then on to 1.3. It's a drawn low-poly Toronto with a CN Tower nod on the waterfront, not a real map. Mint units are let and yellow ones vacant. Tenant pins use initials, never photos.

A

Drone sweep

A high drone orbit dives to one tower, which turns to glass. Units light up floor by floor, tenants pop in and feature cards float out. Ends on the name.

B

Dollhouse

It starts as a flat map seen from above, tilts into 3D and pushes into a building whose floors lift apart like a dollhouse. Ends with Purr cheering.

These are review renders at 720 × 1560. The app gets the chosen fly-over at full resolution. The launch, dots and welcome screen get rebuilt natively in SwiftUI, so they start instantly and calm down when Reduce Motion is on.
