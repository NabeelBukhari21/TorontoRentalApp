Five paw tab bars

Recorded live from the app. Properties sits in the middle on a paw in all five. Watch each tab change, then the scroll at the end, where the bar shrinks and reacts. Haptics can only be felt on your phone.

ALiquid drop

A drop of cherry liquid lives inside the glass pill. It flows to the tab you pick and swells into a paw on Properties. Scrolling shakes a bead loose.

BPaw cradle

The glass paw floats in a dip in the bar, and paw prints walk to the tab you choose. The paw rocks as you scroll. The calmest of the five.

CClay keys

Chunky 3D keys, each in its own colour, that press in with a splash. A wave runs through them as you scroll.

DPurr on patrol

Purr runs along the bar to your tab and acts it out, then ducks behind the glass when you scroll down. The most character.

EBig paw

No bar at all: the navigation is one glass paw, with Properties as the pad and the other tabs as toe beans. It curls up small as you scroll. The boldest, and it covers the most screen.
