Three directions, recorded from the app with the live Purr rig. He acts out each answer, so watch him change when one is picked. Choose one direction for everything, or mix screen by screen.

APaw stage

Purr stands full length on a liquid pool of colour, behind a counter of answer cards. Clay props drift behind him and sit on the counter’s edge. Closest to BitePal, and the easiest to read.

BBig Purr

Purr fills the bottom half of a full-colour screen, with his button on his tummy. Answers are chunky keys that press in. He gets the most screen here, and the building step floods with liquid.

CLiquid orbit

Answers are bubbles floating around Purr, some behind him and some in front. A picked bubble fills and a strand of liquid joins it to his pool. The most playful, with the least room for text.
